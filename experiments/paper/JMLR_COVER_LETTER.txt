Dear Editors,

Please consider "LSREP: A Longitudinal State-Replay Protocol for Evaluating
Conversational Memory, with ICE v2 as an Audited Local-First Architecture"
for publication in JMLR.

LSREP evaluates persistent conversational memory through ordered state replay,
declared lifecycle schedules, repeated probes with evolving reference answers,
and mechanism-fidelity checks. Frozen ICE v2 is an audited case study. The
longitudinal replay and matched public LongMemEval diagnostic expose
complementary failures, including decisive public-diagnostic losses to
vector-RAG. The contribution is an evaluation protocol with explicit validity
and reproducibility boundaries.

Originality and overlap: the work is available as arXiv:2609.16730
(https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16730). It has not appeared in a journal or
conference/workshop proceedings, and there is no overlapping prior refereed
publication. The preprint and public repository contain versions of this same
work. It is not simultaneously under consideration at another journal or
conference. I am the sole author and consent to JMLR review.
