Dear Editors,

Please consider my manuscript, "LSREP: A Longitudinal State-Replay Protocol
for Evaluating Conversational Memory, with ICE v2 as an Audited Local-First
Architecture," for publication in the Journal of Machine Learning Research.

The contribution is an evaluation protocol for persistent conversational
memory: ordered state reconstruction under declared lifecycle schedules,
repeated probes with evolving reference answers, and checks that the mechanisms
credited by a result actually executed. The paper uses frozen ICE v2 as an
audited architectural case study. Its longitudinal replay and matched public
LongMemEval diagnostic expose different failures, including decisive losses
to vector-RAG on the public diagnostic. The manuscript preserves these
negative results and the limits of mechanism attribution. Its purpose is to
advance how memory systems are evaluated, rather than claim a new best-performing
memory system.
