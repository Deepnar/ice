wrong about its own subject, the look-ahead and propagation notes, and the
validation numbers.** Split out of [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) on 2026-08-10,

mostly not queue. Nothing was rewritten or compressed: these are the entries
verbatim, because the forensics in them are the most useful thing the project
has written down. The clearest example is the standing pattern that **seven
consecutive entries have been wrong about their own subject**, which is only
visible because each completion note records which way its entry was wrong.

**How to use it.** Read the stub in ROADMAP.md first; come here when you need
what actually happened. Cross-references of the form `(#g36)` still resolve
inside ROADMAP.md — the stubs carry the anchors — and each stub links here.
